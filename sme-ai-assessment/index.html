01 Your business challenge

Start with a real problem.

Describe the work as it happens today. You can also leave the fields blank and fill in the placeholders directly in your AI chatbot.

A few sentences are enough. Explain what starts the task, who does it, and what takes the most time.
Need an idea?

Think about customer requests, invoicing, inventory, reporting, order processing, appointment scheduling or copying data between tools. Choose one specific workflow rather than your whole business.

03 Bring your findings back
Three things to share in our discussion.
After your chatbot answers, don't worry about presenting the entire analysis. Come back with these three takeaways:
01 / THE OPPORTUNITY
What would you improve?
State your process and the best improvement AI suggested, in one sentence.
02 / THE FIRST STEP
What can you test?
Describe a small pilot your team could realistically try during the next 30 days.
03 / THE REAL QUESTION
What's still uncertain?
Bring one question about cost, feasibility, data, staff adoption or how to get started.


Privacy and accuracy: Use a fictional or anonymized example. Do not paste customer names, personal data, account credentials, confidential contracts or commercially sensitive records. AI recommendations are starting points, not validated cost estimates or implementation guarantees.
