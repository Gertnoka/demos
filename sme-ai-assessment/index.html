01 Your business challenge

What would you like to solve?

Describe one process you want to improve. You can also leave the fields blank and fill in the placeholders inside your AI chatbot.

A few sentences are enough. Mention the tools you use today, the desired result and anything that must remain under human approval.
Need an idea?

Think about customer requests, invoicing, inventory, reporting, order processing, appointment scheduling or copying data between tools. Choose one specific workflow rather than your whole business.

02 Ready for any AI chatbot

Your AI solution prompt

The prompt asks for real tools, an implementation blueprint, a first-day checklist and a pilot plan. Copy it into any AI chatbot.

This page does not send your text anywhere. The AI advice begins only when you paste this prompt into your chatbot.




03 Bring your findings back
Bring back your solution plan.
You do not need to present the full chatbot response. Share these three concrete outcomes:
01 / THE SOLUTION
What will you implement?
Explain the proposed solution in one sentence and name the recommended tools.
02 / FIRST ACTION
What will you do first?
Name one specific setup action you could take this week, and the test you will run.
03 / BLOCKERS
What is holding you back?
Bring one practical blocker such as access, cost, skills, data privacy or approvals.


Privacy and accuracy: Use a fictional or anonymized example. Do not paste customer names, personal data, account credentials, confidential contracts or commercially sensitive records. Verify recommended tool features, prices and integration availability before purchasing or connecting real data.
