Your AI reporting prompt

Act as a business analyst. Analyze the attached Excel file (sheet: Sales_Data) and create a clear, one-page report for a small-business owner. 1. Calculate: total revenue, product costs, gross profit, and gross profit margin (%). 2. Show monthly sales trends and the top 5 products and customers by revenue. 3. Create two charts: monthly sales and top products. 4. Identify 3 meaningful findings, supported by numbers from the file. 5. Recommend 3 specific actions we could take next. For each, explain the first step and how to measure success. Use only the uploaded data. Check calculations, do not invent missing figures, and distinguish product costs from total business expenses. Keep the language simple and the report concise.

Tip: Allow the chatbot to finish analyzing the file before discussing its answer.

Discussion: What would you improve first?
